# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

cooking enthusiasts and home cooks who want to organize recipes and plan meals

## Product Purpose

Transform meal planning from a chore into a creative act with auto-generated shopping lists

## Brand Personality

warm, wholesome, practical

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with purple-to-blue gradients
- Over-designed dashboards where data is buried in decoration
- AI-generated interfaces that all share one look (Inter font, cream backgrounds, rounded everything)
- Enterprise apps that confuse density with professionalism

## Design Principles

- Plan once, shop once — the list should write itself
- Recipes are personal — adjust, annotate, adapt
- Meal planning should feel like anticipation, not admin
- Ingredients connect recipes, categories connect meals

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance target
- Proper color contrast ratios (body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1)
- Keyboard navigable interface
- Screen reader friendly semantic HTML
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme
